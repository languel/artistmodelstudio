# Rights: The silhouette film

The rule for this lesson: **cite the work, make original material, or use what is clearly public domain everywhere.**

- **Drawings and code.** Every shape in the lesson (the puppet, the hills, the reeds, the moon, the lightbox) is drawn in code for this lesson. None is copied from Lotte Reiniger's films or books.
- **The film.** The Adventures of Prince Achmed (1926) is cited, not reproduced. It is in the public domain in the United States, but Reiniger died in 1981, so her work is still protected in Germany and the EU: no frame, design or still is used here.
- **Textures.** The paper fibre, film grain and lamp mask are generated by the code (random noise and gradients).
- **Photographs.** One, by the lesson author's decision: *The Pond—Moonlight* (Edward Steichen, 1904), `media/pond-moonlight.jpg`, a copy at 1400 px wide of [the Wikimedia Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ThePondMoonlight.jpg) (a scan from the Metropolitan Museum of Art; Commons licence: public domain).
  - It is in the public domain in the **United States** (made and published in 1904).
  - It is **not** clear everywhere: Steichen died in 1973, so under the life-plus-70 rule (the EU and others) it is protected until 2043. Anyone using the lesson outside the United States, or publishing it, should check or replace it. Swapping it takes one line: the picture is named `pond-moonlight` and the sketch loads it by name.
  - A further photograph must come from an author who died more than 100 years ago, from a faithful scan, with its source and licence recorded here before it is used.
