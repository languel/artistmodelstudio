# Sample media

- `muybridge-horse.gif`: Eadweard Muybridge, race horse (photographs before 1887, animated 2006). Public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muybridge_race_horse_animated.gif
- `edison-lost-chord.mp3`: *The Lost Chord* (Arthur Sullivan, 1877) on piano and cornet, an Edison "Perfected" Phonograph
  cylinder recorded by George Gouraud in London, 1888; performers unknown. Public domain. Converted from the Ogg Vorbis on
  Wikimedia Commons (from the US National Park Service, Thomas Edison National Historical Park).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Edison_cylinder_Lost_Chord.ogg
- `edison-sullivan.mp3`: Arthur Sullivan's after-dinner message to Edison, recorded by George Gouraud at a phonograph
  party in London, 5 October 1888. Public domain. Converted from the Ogg Vorbis on Wikimedia Commons (same source).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arthur_Sullivan_-_wax_cylinder_recording.ogg
- `voyager-*`: from the Voyager Golden Record (1977) and the Voyager mission, works of NASA, public domain (via Wikimedia
  Commons). Only NASA's own parts are here; the record's music is under other rights and is not included.
  - `voyager-greetings.mp3`: greetings in 55 languages (mono, 64 kb/s).
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voyager_Golden_Record_-_Greetings_In_55_Languages.mp3
  - `voyager-hello.mp3`: "Hello from the children of planet Earth" (Nick Sagan).
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voyager_Golden_Record_greeting_in_English.ogg
  - `voyager-interstellar-plasma.mp3`: Voyager 1's plasma wave instrument, 2012–2013, the sound of NASA/JPL's video.
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voyager_Captures_Sounds_of_Interstellar_Space.webm
  - `voyager-record-cover.jpg` (1200 px): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Sounds_of_Earth_Record_Cover_-_GPN-2000-001978.jpg
  - `voyager-record-cover-explanation.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voyager_Golden_Record_Cover_Explanation.svg
  - `voyager-record.jpg` (1200 px): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Sounds_of_Earth_-_GPN-2000-001976.jpg
  - `voyager-mementos.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PIA17035_Mementos_of_Earth.jpg
- `voyager-jupiter-magnetic-waves.mp3`: plasma waves around Jupiter recorded by Voyager, March 1979, as sound (the first
  2 minutes, mono). NASA, public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jupiter_Magnetic_Waves.webm
- `insight-mars-impact.mp3`: a meteoroid impact on Mars, 24 December 2021, recorded by InSight's seismometer and sped up.
  NASA/JPL-Caltech/CNES/Imperial College London; public domain on Wikimedia Commons (PD-USGov-NASA).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA%E2%80%99s_InSight_Records_the_Sound_of_a_Martian_Impact_(PIA25582).wav
- `arecibo-message.png`, `arecibo-message.json`: the Arecibo message (1974), 1679 bits, as sent (23 × 73). Read from the
  one-pixel-per-bit image by Pengo, CC0 (the message itself is a fact, not a work).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arecibo_message_tiny.png
- `countdown-*.mp4/.gif`: made for this project (numbered frames), see `scripts/`: no rights held.

More samples (ballet, a serpentine dance, Fred Ott's sneeze, the May Irwin kiss) are kept out of the repository in
`examples/media/local/` with their sources and licences in `samples.json`; two of them are CC BY-SA (attribute if shared).
