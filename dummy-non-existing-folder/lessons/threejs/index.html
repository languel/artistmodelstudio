Three.js for body and sound

Part of the Artist–Model Studio lessons. Every lesson page has the same numbered menu at its top: one click to any other.

  1. A spinning cube — the whole program
  2. Anatomy of a scene — scene, camera, objects, materials, light
  3. A body in the scene — MediaPipe landmarks live, from clips, or recorded
  4. Pinboard — person mask and monocular depth drive a pin screen
  5. The body in metres — depth and pose fused into one metric space with a point cloud
  6. Laser harp — beams in the air at measured distances; fingertips pluck notes
  7. Body drum — pads on your own body; hand hits, claps and stomps make drum sounds
  8. Triadic costumes — spheres, spirals, discs and hoops hung on a moving body, after Schlemmer
  9. One line — a face or figure drawn without lifting the pen, after Picasso
  10. Paint marks — hands, feet and head leave strokes in space that you can orbit, after Ghostcatching
  11. Tutorial: one file you can double-click — bundling, embedding, esbuild and blob URLs explained

Models

No model files are stored in the repository. Lessons 02 and up need MediaPipe models; 03–05 also the depth model. Fetch them once (about 145 MB; after that nothing leaves your machine):

npm run lessons:models

or sh lessons/threejs/fetch-mediapipe.sh. Or download the files yourself into the folders shown. A missing model shows a red dot; reload this page after fetching.
