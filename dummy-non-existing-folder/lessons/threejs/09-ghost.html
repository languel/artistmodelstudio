What it is

Each chosen point (hands, feet, head, elbows, knees) is followed after the cleaning of lesson 07. While it moves it lays down a stroke: positions, times and speeds. A stroke becomes a ribbon that always faces you, wide where the point moved slowly and narrow where it moved fast. When a point stops, or is lost, the pen lifts.

Everything is kept in space, so you can pause and orbit the painting. Ghostcatching (1999) did this with motion-capture data of Bill T. Jones dancing; this is a study in that spirit, not a copy of it.
