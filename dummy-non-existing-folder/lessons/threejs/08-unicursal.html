One line

A face or a figure drawn without lifting the pen, after Picasso's one-line drawings. The line is redrawn every frame from your body, and it lives in 3D: orbit it.

What it is

A stroke is a run of landmarks (an eye, an outline). Each is smoothed into a curve, the strokes are put in an order, and the gaps are filled with curves that leave the way the pen was heading, so the whole drawing is one path. The pen then adds a little wobble, swells and thins, and tapers at the ends.

It began as an accident: the face tracker drew the 1896 kiss as two overlapping faces, each feature one stroke, and it looked like a Picasso. Try the kiss clip with copies set to 2.
