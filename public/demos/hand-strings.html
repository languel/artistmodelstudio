Strings in the Air

A mirror you can play. Pinch with both hands and pull to stretch a plucked string between them; join your thumbs and index fingers (a ring, a heart) to make a drum or bell. Swipe a fingertip across a string to pluck it, poke a pad to hit it, pinch one to move it.

Every note comes from where the thing is, snapped to a scale you choose, so it always sounds in tune. Your face plays too: open your mouth for a brighter sound, raise your brows for more echo, smile for vibrato, close your eyes for long sustain.

The camera stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Tuning and view
Strings: height → note on the scale, length → octave (shorter is higher; swap in “Pitch from”), angle → tone (level: bright steel, upright: dark nylon), left–right → stereo, where you pluck → timbre.
Pads: size → note (bigger is lower), height → material (glass, bell, vibes, marimba, drum from top to bottom), where you hit → centre is pure, edge is ringing.
Two-hand pinch, pull apart, let go → a string · thumbs + index fingers together (hold) → a pad, as big as the ring · swipe a fingertip across a string → pluck · poke a pad → hit · one-hand pinch on an end, a string or a pad → move it; drop it on ✕ to delete.
Mouse: drag → string · shift-drag → pad · move across strings → pluck · click a pad → hit · double-click → delete · H hides this.
