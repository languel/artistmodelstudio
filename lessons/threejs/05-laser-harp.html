What it is

Each beam is a line from an emitter on the floor, in metres from the camera. A hand interrupts a beam when it is at the plane in depth and on the beam sideways. The camera alone cannot tell touching the plane from being in front of it; the metric depth of lesson 04 can.

The note starts when the beam is cut and stays while it is held, fading in over the attack and out over the release. The height of the cut along the beam moves the filter.
