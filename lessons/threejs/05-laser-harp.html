What it is

Every beam is a vertical line at a position in metres. A hand crosses the harp's plane when its distance from the camera passes the plane's distance. That needs the body in metres from lesson 04: the camera alone only knows left and right.

Depth is good to a few centimetres, so a pluck needs the fingertip to get clearly (3 cm) through the plane. Faster means louder.
