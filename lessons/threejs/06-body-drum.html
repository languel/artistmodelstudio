What it is

A pad is a sphere attached to the body by mixing joints (the chest is mostly shoulders, the belly mostly hips), so it moves with you. A hit is a hand arriving: it has to enter the pad and be closing on it, relative to the body's own motion, faster than the sensitivity. That speed is the loudness.

Everything is measured in metres around your hips, scaled by your shoulder width, so it needs no depth and works at any distance. The strip at the bottom is the last eight seconds, one row per pad: the score you just played. An audio-visual study after Laurie Anderson's body drum, not a copy of it.
