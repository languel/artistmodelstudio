Pinboard

A pin-screen toy: every pin is pushed out by what the camera sees. Two estimates drive it, a mask (which pixels are the person) and a depth (how far each pixel is).

What it is

One InstancedMesh of shafts and one of heads, a few thousand pins, each placed by a height from two arrays. Nothing here is a mesh of the person: the person is only the pattern in the heights.

The depth is relative (near vs far), not metres. Lesson 04 anchors it to the body's measured size.
