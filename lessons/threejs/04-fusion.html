What it is

Three facts become one space. MediaPipe knows the body's shape in metres. A limb's apparent size gives its distance. The depth map gives near and far for every pixel, and the joints' known distances turn that into metres.

It is an estimate, good to a few centimetres on a good day. The measured numbers show how well the depth map agrees with the body.
