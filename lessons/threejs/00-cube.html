A spinning cube

The whole program. This is the file running on the right (cube.js). Everything else in three.js is a variation on these four things.


  
Four things

  
Scene holds what exists. Camera says where we look from. Mesh (geometry + material) is a thing to look at. Renderer draws the scene through the camera onto a canvas, sixty times a second.

  
Change the numbers in cube.js and reload: the rotation speeds, camera.position.z, the box size.
