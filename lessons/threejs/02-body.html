What it is

A landmark is a labelled 3D point. We keep them in a Body and draw it with a BodyView: joints are instanced spheres, bones are one LineSegments, the face is one Points.

body.joint('leftWrist')          // scene position
body.speed[J.rightAnkle]         // metres per second
body.distance('leftWrist','nose')// metres

Joint size and colour follow speed. The same Body will feed the drum, the costumes and the paint.
